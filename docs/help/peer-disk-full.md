# The other device's disk is full

## What happened
The device receiving the files ran out of space, and told this one, so sending stopped.

## Why
The files are bigger than the space left there. Everything that arrived before is kept
there, verified.

## What to do
1. Free some space on the receiving device. On a phone: Settings, Storage. On a computer:
   empty the trash, or remove files you don't need.
2. Send the same files again. The transfer continues where it stopped; nothing that
   arrived is sent twice.

If the receiving device is a computer, its glft log names the file that didn't fit.
